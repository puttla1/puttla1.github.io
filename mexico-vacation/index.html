The short hike

Chipinque

Easy walk above San Pedro

A protected forest in the Sierra Madre, right above the InterContinental's neighborhood. It has more than 60 km of marked trails. Pick a short loop from the main area, then stop at the La Peñita overlook and the bridge at km 4.

Skip the summit routes (La M, Copete de las Águilas). They need a separate permit, start early in the morning, and can take most of a day.

Before you go

Need to know

Closures

Historia Mexicana, Noreste and Palacio closed Dec 24, 25, 31 and Jan 1 last season, and they close Mondays. MARCO also closes Mondays. Plan museums for Saturday and Sunday.

Safety

The State Department lists Nuevo León at Level 2, "exercise increased caution." Use app rides or dispatched taxis, and don't drive between cities after dark.

Getting around

Uber and DiDi are allowed at MTY. Normal fares to San Pedro run about MXN 200–380, but surge on NYE. The authorized airport taxi to San Pedro is about MXN 900.

Bags on Viva

The base fare includes only a personal item. Carry-ons and checked bags cost extra, and fees are higher at the airport than online.

Weather

January averages about 50°F lows and 72°F highs. A cold front can drop the city into the 30s, and Chipinque runs colder than town.

Sundays are free

The three state museums and MARCO charge nothing on Sundays, and the state museums stay open until 8 PM.
