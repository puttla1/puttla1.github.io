Barbecue, with real options for vegetarians

Most Texas BBQ joints have only a few meat-free sides, and beans often have pork in them. Friday lunch is all plant-based, and the Saturday grill is chicken and veggies only.

Mission Burger Co.

1200 Barbara Jordan Blvd, Mueller, Austin · Friday lunch

A plant-based burger spot that has done a smoky mushroom “brisket” burger and a BBQ plate, so the vegetarians still get BBQ flavor. Open 11 AM–11 PM daily.

Vegetarian: the whole menu. Check the current menu for the BBQ items.

Curry Boys BBQ

536 E Courtland Pl, San Antonio · open 11–10 daily

Texas smoked meats served with Southeast Asian curries, like brisket with green curry. It's fun for a birthday group, and they serve beer and wine.

Vegetarian: not confirmed. Call (210) 560-2763 and ask about meat-free curries before you commit.
