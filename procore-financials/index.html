Layout

Statements are from Procore’s Form 10-K for fiscal 2025 (income statement and cash flows for FY2023–FY2025, balance sheets at December 31, 2025 and 2024) and Form 10-K for fiscal 2024 (balance sheet at December 31, 2023). Free cash flow is operating cash flow less purchases of property and equipment and capitalized software, computed from the 10-K; the FY2025 earnings release showed $215.1M before the 10-K moved $1.4M of deferred acquisition payments into financing. Non-GAAP figures, stock-based compensation by line, customer counts, retention, and RPO come from Procore’s fourth-quarter earnings releases. FY2022 figures used for growth rates and averages come from the FY2024 10-K and the Q4 2023 earnings release.

Sources: FY2025 Form 10-K, FY2024 Form 10-K, Q4 2025 earnings release, Q4 2024 earnings release, Q4 2023 earnings release. Public information only; for learning, not investment advice.
