Good to know

Delhi fog

Early February mornings can be foggy, which delays flights. The Feb 10 flight lands at 8:25 AM, four hours before the 12:30 PM haldi. If it looks bad on Tuesday night, check later morning flights right away.

IndiGo bag limit

IndiGo’s domestic economy allowance is usually 15 kg per checked bag, lighter than Turkish. If you’re carrying wedding outfits, prepay extra weight online. It costs much more at the airport.

Bags in Istanbul

On the way out, collect your bags in Istanbul and check them again Monday evening. On the way home, you’ll clear customs and recheck them in Houston.

Paying in India

Use the Sapphire Reserve and always choose to pay in rupees. Paying in dollars gives a worse exchange rate.

Time zones

Istanbul is 9 hours ahead of Texas. India is 11½ hours ahead.

Delhi airport terminals

IndiGo uses more than one terminal in Delhi. Check which one is on your booking the night before.

Sights

Istanbul one day, Monday

  • Hagia Sophia Visitor entry is ticketed, and it closes to visitors during prayer times.
  • Blue Mosque Cover shoulders and knees. Women will need a headscarf, and loaners are usually available.
  • Grand Bazaar Closed Sundays, so Monday works well.
  • Bosphorus cruise Best in the afternoon light. Public ferries are a cheap alternative.
  • Galata and Karaköy Good for dinner on Sunday night, near the hotel.

Agra Tuesday

  • Taj Mahal Closed Fridays. Bring only a small bag; large bags and food aren’t allowed in.
  • Agra Fort A short drive from the Taj. Allow about an hour.
  • Mehtab Bagh Gardens across the river with a view of the Taj, if there’s time.
